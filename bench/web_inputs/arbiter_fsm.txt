A state machine for a simple two-master round-robin bus arbiter. Draw it as states and
transitions, not as a floorplan.

There are four states, drawn as circles:
  "Idle"
  "Grant A"
  "Grant B"
  "Park"

Transitions, each with its own label:
  Idle -> Grant A, labelled "req A only"
  Idle -> Grant B, labelled "req B only"
  Grant A -> Grant B, labelled "req B, A done"
  Grant B -> Grant A, labelled "req A, B done"
  Grant A -> Idle, labelled "A done, no req"
  Grant B -> Idle, labelled "B done, no req"
  Idle -> Park, labelled "idle timeout"
  Park -> Idle, labelled "any request"

Two transitions return to the state they came from:
  Grant A -> Grant A, labelled "A still asserted"
  Grant B -> Grant B, labelled "B still asserted"
