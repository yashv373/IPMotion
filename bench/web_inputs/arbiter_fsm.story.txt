Walk through one round of arbitration.

request asserted for another cycle, so the machine stays in Grant A. Then B requests while A
finishes, so the arbiter hands over to Grant B. B finishes with nothing else pending, so it
returns to Idle, and after a quiet spell it drops into Park.

Highlight the state the machine is in at each step, and flash the transition being taken.
