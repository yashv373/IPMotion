The PULP platform family, drawn as four labelled groups stacked down the frame, with an IoT-to-HPC spectrum
arrow across the middle.

Group 1, across the top left, titled "RISC-V Cores and Vector Units":
  RI5CY (CV32E, RV32)
  Zero R (Ibex, RV32)
  Snitch (RV32)
  Spatz (RVV)
  Ariane (CVA6, RV64)
  ARA (RVV)

Group 2, top middle, titled "Peripherals":
  JTAG, SPI
  UART, I2S
  DMA, GPIO

Group 3, top right, titled "Interconnects":
  LIC, HCI
  APB, FlooNoC
  AXI4

Group 4, a wide band under the first three, titled "Platforms". It holds three example systems side by side,
each one a small cluster of tiles around a vertical interconnect bar:
  left:   "Single core" - tiles I, O, M, R5, A around one interconnect bar.
          Named systems: PULPino, PULPissimo, Cheshire
  middle: "Multi-core" - tiles M, I, O, R5 around an interconnect bar, next to a "cluster" box holding
          four M tiles over a horizontal interconnect bar over A, R5, R5, R5.
          Named systems: OpenPULP, ControlPULP
  right:  "Heterogeneous, Many-core" - the same arrangement as Multi-core, with the cluster box repeated
          three times behind itself to show many of them.
          Named systems: Hero, Carfield, Astral, Occamy, Mempool

A wide left-to-right arrow runs under the Platforms band, labelled "IOT" at its left end and "HPC" at its
right end. It is a spectrum: the three platform groups above it get bigger from left to right.

Group 5, along the bottom, titled "Accelerators and ISA extensions":
  XpulpNN, XpulpTNN
  ITA (Transformers)
  RBE, NEUREKA (QNNs)
  FFT (DSP)
  REDMULE (FP-Tensor)

Wiring:
  There are no wires between the groups. The only connecting shapes are the vertical interconnect bars inside
  each platform, which touch the tiles on either side of them, and the horizontal interconnect bar inside each
  cluster box.
